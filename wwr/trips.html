Scenic Family Float

This relaxing trip is perfect for families, first-time rafters, and visitors who want to enjoy mountain views, wildlife, and calm sections of the river. Guides provide basic instruction and help guests feel comfortable on the water.

Canyon Rapids Run

Our most popular adventure combines beautiful canyon scenery with exciting beginner and intermediate rapids. This trip is a great choice for groups who want a mix of fun, challenge, and time to enjoy the river.

Thunder Gorge Challenge

This high-energy trip is designed for adventurous guests who are ready for stronger rapids and a faster pace. Guides review safety procedures before the trip and lead the group through an unforgettable white water route.
