Family Float

This gentle half-day trip is designed for families, first-time rafters, and groups who want beautiful scenery with mild rapids. Guides teach basic paddling and point out wildlife along the river.

  • Skill level: Beginner
  • Length: Half day
  • Best for: Families and first-time rafters

Canyon Rush

Canyon Rush blends scenic canyon walls with steady Class III rapids. It is a great choice for adventurous beginners or returning guests who want more excitement without committing to the most difficult route.

  • Skill level: Intermediate
  • Length: Full day
  • Best for: Active groups and adventurous beginners

Thunder Falls Expedition

This advanced route is made for experienced rafters looking for fast water, technical paddling, and a full day of high-energy teamwork. Previous rafting experience is recommended for this trip.

  • Skill level: Advanced
  • Length: Full day
  • Best for: Experienced rafters and thrill seekers

Available Equipment

Rafting Gear

Rafts, paddles, helmets, and U.S. Coast Guard-approved life jackets are provided for every trip.

Safety Support

Each trip includes guide instruction, safety talks, throw bags, and first-aid supplies.

Optional Items

Dry bags, splash jackets, and wetsuits are available when weather or water conditions require them.
